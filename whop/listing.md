## English

**Product name:** Family Travel Tracker — Complete Travel SaaS (Next.js 15 + Supabase)

**Tagline:** A polished, animated, multi-language family travel map — sell it to your clients or run it as your own product.

**Description:**

Every family that travels wants one thing: to SEE their journeys. Family Travel Tracker turns trips into a glowing, color-coded world map — one color per family member, with dates, notes, a bucket list, and a shareable public page.

This is a complete, production-grade codebase — not a tutorial project:

🌍 **Complete world map** — all 173 countries, per-member colors, staggered glow-in animation, hover tooltips with flags (the original free maps miss ~27 major countries — this one is generated from real geodata)

👨‍👩‍👧 **Family profiles** — unlimited members, each with their own color; view one member or everyone at once

📊 **Stats dashboard** — animated count-ups, % of the world ring, family leaderboard, per-continent breakdown

⭐ **Bucket list** — wishlist countries with dashed map outlines, one-tap "went there" conversion

🔗 **Viral share pages** — every user gets a public `/u/username` map that needs no login

🎉 **Delight built in** — confetti on every new country, milestone celebrations, aurora design system

🌐 **Trilingual out of the box** — English, Russian, Armenian (framework ready for more)

🔐 **Modern stack** — Next.js 15 App Router, React 18, TypeScript, Tailwind, framer-motion, Supabase (Postgres + Auth with row-level security). Runs entirely on the public anon key — no server secrets.
